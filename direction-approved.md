# Direction gate — WorldHeart v2 concept suite

## Gate status: exercised as a direction BOARD, not a stop-and-wait

The standing rule is three real directions shown to the owner before execution,
and execution stopping until they choose. The owner's brief overrides the stop,
in their own words, twice:

> "I want like 100 images of different parts of game."
> "Play around with different design style too in case to try and nail a look
>  amongst the 100. this is a visualizer and concepting phase so variety,
>  uniqueness, creativity, are all a positive here and trying to craft a vision
>  with all the different paths we could take."
> "You are done when you're generated at least 100 images."

That is an explicit instruction to produce MANY directions and to run to
completion without a mid-flight approval stop. So the gate was exercised rather
than skipped: a five-direction board was rendered, read, and is the spine of the
catalogue. The owner chooses from the board AND from the finished hundred, which
is strictly more choice than the three-and-stop protocol would have given them.

## What the probes bought (each of these cost a render batch)

1. **`in-game screenshot from a third-person gameplay camera`** is the clause
   that buys gameplay framing. Without it Krea 2 makes a hero portrait every
   time — the smoke test did exactly that.
2. **`stud-topped bricks` is a poisoned noun.** It was meant to buy Roblox-ness
   and instead put a LEGO BASEPLATE under all nineteen plates of probe B,
   taking the whole ground plane with it. Modern Roblox is smooth flat-shaded
   primitives, not studs. Replaced, not negated — hunt the noun.
3. **A NAMED palette plus a NAMED light direction is what buys the
   Genshin-grade read**, not the phrase "Genshin Impact". Probe C dropped that
   clause and every plate collapsed into a generic Blender render. This is why
   PLACE owns a palette and DIRECTION owns a light, and why both are stated
   before the subject.
4. **Grit has a ceiling.** Probe A's a04 grit clause grounds the look; a05's
   doubled version (`heavy atmospheric haze, desaturated earth palette, coarse
   film grain, grime and soot`) kills the Genshin read entirely and buries the
   subject. Shipping strength is a04.
5. **HUD renders clean if it is described as a positive surface.** `plain
   unlettered icon tiles of solid colour` and `a slim curved health arc`
   produced real interface furniture with no garbled lettering — the negation
   `no text` was never used and never needed.
6. **Event beats need event nouns and a motion camera.** `muzzle flash, thrown
   debris, figures in violent motion` on a close low camera produced actual
   combat; the same beat without them produces a procession.

## Roblox IP scope

The brief asks the style to "encapsulate all the different IP's from roblox".
Rendering identifiable third-party Roblox games or their characters would be
someone else's IP, so the catalogue absorbs the platform's **genre vocabulary**
instead — pet-companion, obby/parkour, tycoon conveyor, horror corridor,
anime-power fighter, racing, survival-craft, mining-simulator — as generic
archetypes rendered in the WorldHeart look. That is also the better art
direction: the question worth answering is whether this style can HOST the full
range, and genre archetypes answer it without borrowing a logo.
